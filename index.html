OLIVER CHILD

Researching and practicing the fabrication of delightful technology

I'm a postdoc in the Human Computer Interaction group at the Hasso Plattner Institute, working on digital fabrication: how everyday tools like 3D printers can help people make interactive, electronic objects. Previously I did my PhD in the Bristol Interaction Group at the University of Bristol.
